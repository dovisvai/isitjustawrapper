# Code of conduct

This project publishes opinions about how other people price their products. That guarantees disagreement, and a good
deal of it will come from people whose livelihood is attached to the thing being discussed. The standard here exists
for that situation specifically.

## What is expected

**Argue about the work, not the person.** "This assumption is unrepresentative because most customers do X" is the
argument. "You clearly have never built anything" is not.

**Be specific.** A claim that a verdict is unfair carries no weight without saying what the price buys that the entry
missed. Vagueness is not politeness.

**Disclose your interest.** If you work for a vendor being discussed, say so. Nobody is excluded for it — vendor
corrections are among the most useful contributions this project gets — but an undisclosed stake poisons the record.

**Separate facts from opinions.** A wrong price gets fixed on sight and does not require a debate. A verdict is
opinion, and arguing about it is legitimate and welcome. Conflating the two wastes everyone's time.

**Assume the mistake was honest.** This project has published its own errors, at length, on a public page. Errors here
are errors, not malice, and the same courtesy is extended to everyone.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about anyone
- Discrimination or slurs of any kind
- Publishing anyone's private information
- Legal threats used to shut down discussion rather than correct a fact. If something here is factually wrong, say what
  and we will fix it — that route is open, fast, and free
- Sustained bad-faith argument after a factual point has been conceded

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to issues, pull requests, commits, and any other project space.

Report a problem to <office@isitjustawrapper.com>. Reports are handled privately. Depending on severity, the response ranges from a
warning to a permanent ban from the repository.

Maintainers who break this standard are held to it more strictly than anyone else, not less. A project whose entire
argument is about honesty does not get to be selectively honest about its own conduct.
